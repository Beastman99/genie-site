Why Genie exists

Why standard reports stop at medical results

IVF clinics focus on embryo health, viability and inherited disease risks. Standard reports summarise the medical results.

Traits like height, eye colour, IQ and temperament-linked tendencies create ethical and counselling complexity for clinics, so they stay outside the standard report.

Genie turns the raw genetic data from your clinic into a clear, expanded trait report for parents.
